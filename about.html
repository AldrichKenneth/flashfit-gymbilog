ABOUT THE PROJECT

FlashFit

A wellness-integrated flashcard study platform built for Filipino students who deserve more than just another study tool.

BUILT BY GYM BILOG
The Problem

Students deserve better than burnout.

Filipino high school and college students increasingly depend on digital platforms as alternatives to outdated learning materials and limited educational resources. Yet every platform available today focuses exclusively on academic performance, treating the well-being of its users as an afterthought.

Students who rely on these tools often study in long, unbroken sessions that quietly accumulate eye strain and mental fatigue. The situation is made worse by the added pressure of daily long commutes and heavy academic loads that leave little room for recovery between sessions.

No widely accessible digital study platform integrates wellness support into the learning experience, leaving students to treat academic performance and personal well-being as two entirely separate concerns. FlashFit was built to close that gap.

The Gap

No widely accessible platform integrates wellness support into the digital learning experience for students.

The Cost

Students are left to treat academic performance and personal well-being as two entirely separate concerns.

Our Answer

FlashFit unifies both into a single platform designed around the whole student, not just their grades.

Key Features

Everything you need, in one place.

Flashcard Study

Build custom decks for any subject and study through a flip-based card interface. The hearts system keeps you honest: mark a card wrong and you lose a heart. Restore them through exercise breaks to keep your session going.

Pomodoro Focus

Stay in the zone with customizable timed study blocks. FlashFit structures your session into focused intervals separated by short breaks, helping you work with your attention span instead of pushing through exhaustion.

Wellness System

Built-in wellness features run quietly alongside your study session. The 20-20-20 eye care timer fires every 20 minutes, exercise breaks restore lost hearts, and the Wellness Hub tracks your mood, streak, and activity over time.

How It Works

Four steps to a better study session.

1

Build a Deck

Add study cards organized by subject. Create as many decks as you need and edit them at any time from the dashboard.

2

Start a Session

Launch the Pomodoro timer and pick the deck you want to review. Set your own focus and break durations to match your pace.

3

Study with Hearts

Flip through your cards. Mark what you know, and lose a heart for every card you get wrong. Honesty keeps you sharp.

4

Take a Break

When the timer ends, complete a short exercise to restore your hearts, rest your eyes, and reset your focus for the next block.

Built With

Pure web. No frameworks.

FlashFit is built entirely with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. State persists across sessions using the browser's localStorage API. No build tools, no dependencies, no frameworks. Just clean, intentional code designed to run in any browser without setup or installation.
